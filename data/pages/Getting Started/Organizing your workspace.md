# Organizing your workspace

The sidebar is your map. It holds your **workspaces**, **folders** and
**pages**, plus favorites, tags and the tools.

## Workspaces, folders and pages

- A **workspace** is a top‑level area (like this "Getting Started" one). On
  disk it's a folder directly inside `data/pages/`.
- A **folder** groups pages inside a workspace.
- A **page** is a single Markdown document.

Create any of them with the **+** button in the sidebar, or from a workspace's
own page. Rename, move, duplicate and delete are all in the right‑click menu
(and the `…` menu on each row) — and every change is made to the real files.

## Find things fast

- **Search** (sidebar or `Ctrl`/`⌘ K`) matches titles, headings, body text and
  tags.
- The **command palette** (`Ctrl`/`⌘ K`) also jumps to pages and runs actions
  like creating a page or opening a tool.
- **Favorites** — star a page to pin it to the top of the sidebar.

## Importing and exporting

Use **Import Markdown** (Home or a workspace) to bring in existing `.md` files.
Any page can be exported from its menu. Because pages are just files, you can
also drop `.md` files straight into `data/pages/` and refresh.
